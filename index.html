What the grading report says

The four Cs are how every lab describes a diamond. Here is what each one means for the stone you will wear.

Cut

How well the facets return light. Of the four, it has the largest effect on sparkle. We recommend Excellent or Ideal for round stones.

Color

Graded D to Z by how little tint a stone shows. D to F are colorless; G to J are near-colorless and look white once set in a ring.

Clarity

The scale runs from Flawless to Included. VS1 and VS2 stones are typically clean to the naked eye, so you do not pay for perfection you cannot see.

Carat

A measure of weight: one carat is 0.2 grams. Price jumps at round numbers, so a 0.9 ct stone can look the same as a 1.0 ct for much less.

Your choice, stated plainly

Natural or lab-grown, always labelled

Natural

Formed in the earth over billions of years. Each stone is sourced under the Kimberley Process and graded by an independent lab.

Lab-grown

Real diamond, grown in a lab. Chemically and optically the same as natural, usually priced lower, and never described as anything else.
